Welcome to my page! My name is Lee Mariault and I am a **postdoctoral researcher** at the IRD (Research Institute for Development) in Montpellier, France. My current research focuses on the impact of **malaria in pregnancy on the fetal immune system**, specifically investigating how placental infection reshapes the transcriptional landscape of cord blood monocytes.

I conducted my PhD at the Laboratory of Genome and Development of Plants (LGDP) in Perpignan, France, where I studied **horizontal transfer events** within thousands of plant and fungal genomes through a massive **comparative phylogenomics** approach.

In addition to my expertise in comparative genomics, I also gained experience in analyzing **single-cell transcriptomics** in leukemia (T-ALL) as well as **structural variants** during prior work experiences.

More about my work can be found in the [publications]({{ base_path }}/publications/) and [talks]({{ base_path }}/talks/) sections, and in my [CV]({{ base_path }}/cv/).
